## Projects

- [Solace Health · Evidence Review (Statistics & A/B testing)](https://github.com/denisetirrel-ops/solace-ab-testing) – Python and SciPy on 1,200 clinic visits: Downtown keeps its 15-minute promise on average, Riverside waits 8.6 minutes longer (Cohen's d = 1.04), and a planned SMS test would prove nothing as designed. Includes a valid redesign with about 470 appointments per group.
- [Health Data Explorer (Python)](https://github.com/denisetirrel-ops/health-data-explorer) – exploratory analysis of 50,001 people with pandas, seaborn, SciPy and scikit-learn: body fat turned out to be calculated, not measured, and smokers only look healthier because 61% of them are men.
- [PeakFlow Online Sales 2024](https://github.com/denisetirrel-ops/peakflow-sales-dashboard) – Power BI dashboard: messy CSV cleaned in Power Query, star schema, DAX measures, and a quality check that caught revenue counted four times.
- [Unicorn Sales Analysis (SQL)](https://github.com/denisetirrel-ops/unicorn-sales-analysis-sql) – PostgreSQL analysis of 9,997 order lines with CTEs and window functions: West grows fastest, and every discount above 20% loses money.
- [Business Cockpit](https://github.com/denisetirrel-ops/business-cockpit) · [live demo](https://tirrel-business-dashboard.streamlit.app) – bilingual (DE/EN) Streamlit app on the same data: plan vs. actual, 13-week cash forecast, Bundesbank benchmark and what-if scenarios for small retailers and their advisors. Every key figure is calculated in Python and SQL and tested against each other.
- [Masterschool → Notion (Chrome extension)](https://github.com/denisetirrel-ops/masterschool-notion-extension) – JavaScript extension (Manifest V3) that copies course lessons into Notion through the Notion API, with code, formulas and images. Write-up of three bugs and how I fixed them.
